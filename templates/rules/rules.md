# Hinterlands Campaign: Complete Opposing Force (OpFor) & Arena Generation Rules

**Direct Verdict / Fix**
This section provides the definitive mechanical framework for generating randomized Opposing Forces (OpFor) and Arena Gladiators for your Hinterlands campaign. It includes force modifiers, mercenary unit generation, comprehensive faction-specific deployment pools, and specialized Game Worlds dueling rules. Use these tables to dynamically generate balanced or asymmetrical threats for Classic BattleTech or Alpha Strike scenarios.
